What a margin is

How far ahead the winner was, in percentage points. R +5 means the Republican got 5 points more than the Democrat. D +5 means the Democrat did.

Why midterms matter

A midterm is an election between presidential years (2018, 2022, 2026). The fairest test of 2026 is to compare it with the last midterm, 2022, not with the 2024 presidential race.

Split tickets

Voting for a Democrat in one race and a Republican in another on the same ballot. All three counties do this a lot.

Every number was matched to an official results page, a local news report or a reference table. Each figure links to its source. Anything we could not confirm was left out.

The trend: Obama counties that became Trump counties

Robeson and Macomb voted for Obama twice. Cambria voted for him once, in 2008. All three then voted for Trump in 2016, 2020 and 2024.

North Carolina

Robeson County: the biggest swing

Trump won Robeson by 27.6 points in 2024. In 2026 the Republican Senate candidate, Michael Whatley, won it by 128 votes, while Democrat Roy Cooper won the seat statewide with 58.8%. On the same ballot, voters re-elected the Democratic sheriff with 74.6%.

What stands out

  • 7,505 more votes for the Democratic sheriff than for the Democratic Senate candidate on the same ballots.
  • Democratic turnout grew. Cooper got about 35% more votes than the 2022 Democrat (10,736 to 14,488). The Republican got about 7% fewer (15,732 to 14,616).
  • Two Republican commissioners lost. Both had switched from Democrat to Republican in 2025. A third Republican seat is 3 votes behind and under protest. Border Belt Independent
  • Registered Democrats keep shrinking, from 33,475 in November 2024 to 29,686 in November 2026, yet the Democratic Senate vote went up.

Pennsylvania

Cambria County: still solidly Republican

Republican Stacy Garrity won Cambria for governor with 55.8% while losing the state by about 22 points. The local paper said the area stayed politically unchanged. The exception is Democrat Frank Burns, who won a 10th term in the state House.

Michigan

Macomb County: a split ticket, again

Macomb picked Democrat Jocelyn Benson for governor and Republican Mike Rogers for U.S. Senate on the same ballot. Benson won statewide with 54.6%, and Democrat Abdul El-Sayed won the Senate seat statewide even though he lost Macomb. Macomb backed the Democrat for governor in 2018, 2022 and 2026, and Trump in 2016, 2020 and 2024.

Summary

What the overall results add up to

All three counties pulled back from their 2024 Trump highs, but none fully reversed. Robeson moved a long way and nearly flipped. Cambria moved a little and stayed firmly Republican. Macomb split its ticket, as it usually does in midterms.

  1. 1. The Trump trend through 2024 was real.Trump's share rose each time in Robeson (50.8%, 58.9%, 63.4%) and Cambria (66.5%, 68.0%, 69.3%). Macomb held near 53.5% twice, then rose to 55.9%.
  2. 2. 2026 was a pullback, not a reversal.Republicans still won the top race in Robeson (by 128 votes) and Cambria (by 6,500 votes). Only Macomb elected a Democrat at the top, and even there the Republican won the Senate race by 7,444 votes.
  3. 3. Only Robeson really moved since 2022.Robeson 18.1 → D Cambria 3.6 → D Macomb 1.6 → R
  4. 4. Registration pointed the wrong way.Robeson lost 3,789 registered Democrats from 2024 to 2026, yet its Democratic Senate vote rose by a third from 2022. Cambria's Republican registration lead grew while its Republican margin shrank.
  5. 5. Voters split tickets for local Democrats they know.Robeson's sheriff got 74.6%. Macomb's Mark Hackel got about 61% for the fifth time. Cambria's Frank Burns won by 17.5 points in a district Trump carried by 31.
  6. 6. Who voted mattered as much as who switched.In Robeson, Democratic votes rose 35% from 2022 while Republican votes fell 7%. Turnout there was only 38.8%.

Still open As of November 19, 2026: the State Senate District 24 recount (Republican leads by 225) and the Robeson commission District 6 protest (Democrat leads by 3) had no reported outcome. Cambria's results are still marked unofficial. Macomb's are official.
